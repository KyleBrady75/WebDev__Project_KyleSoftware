About

Welcome to Wheels On Fire! Your number one automotive information website.
My name is John, the founder. My collection is the greatest in the world, and my knowledge is even greater. With this knowledge, I want to help YOU find the perfect car that will fit all your needs.

Why did I start "Wheels On Fire"?

As I was in the automotive industry for so long, I gained massive amounts of knowledge on these incredible machines. But what is the point of having all this knowledge if I am the only person to benefit from it?
This is where "Wheels on Fire" comes in. This website is completely free to use and allows you to find your perfect car.
As well as that, you can browse the amazing collection I have of my own, and everything about them.

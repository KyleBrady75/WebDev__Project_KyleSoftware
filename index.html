Car of the week:

The BMW M2 is a lightweight sports car for newer drivers looking for track experience.
Don't let its smaller size fool you, because with the right driver, this car can easily beat your Ferrari or Porsche.

  • Engine: 3.0L Twin-Turbo I6
  • Horsepower: 480 hp
  • Top Speed: 250 km/h
  • Price: €35,000
